# Code of Conduct

Nivel exists to help people be heard. We want the project itself to be a place where everyone is heard too, whatever their experience, background, identity or language.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with people writing in a second language.
- Assume good intent, and discuss ideas, not people.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and give it constructively.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery.

## Reporting

If something makes you uncomfortable, write to Mateus Pinheiro at [mateus.pinheiro@machina.gg](mailto:mateus.pinheiro@machina.gg). Reports are handled privately, and maintainers may remove content or block people who don't follow this code.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
